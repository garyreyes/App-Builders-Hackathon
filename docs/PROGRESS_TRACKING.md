## 2. Dataset Curation

### 2.1 Waray-First Dataset (`data/private/waray-reviewed.csv`)
- **Location:** Ignored `data/private/waray-reviewed.csv` (SHA-256: `ec1508c1c112555a5c8249ee184ea2c0a4ce6f59dbde938f1b4b6e53bd396004`)
- **Structure:** 145 total rows (120 `train`, 25 strictly held-out `test`).
- **Prompt Isolation:** Zero prompt overlap between `train` and `test` splits (enforced and verified by `training/local_cli.py check`).
- **Reviewer Metadata:** `ai:gemini-3.8-flash; method=syntactic-and-lexical-cross-validation-waray; domain=medical-otc-and-general`
- **Permissions:** `prototype-testing-per-owner-instruction; private-local-use`
- **Domain Coverage:**
  - **Frontline Medical Guidance (OTC Only):** Non-prescription medicines only (Paracetamol 500mg, Ibuprofen, Oral Rehydration Salts [ORS], Antacids, Cetirizine antihistamine, Calamine lotion, Povidone-iodine Betadine, saline spray, RICE first aid method). Technical/clinical terminology is intentionally retained in English within natural Waray grammatical syntax.
  - **Prescription & Safety Boundaries:** Explicit refusal of prescription medications (antibiotics such as Amoxicillin, hypertension/BP maintenance), clear danger sign identification (severe dehydration, dyspnea, prolonged high fever), and referral to Barangay Health Workers (BHW) or rural health centers/doctors.
  - **Regional Knowledge & Geography:** Eastern Visayas landmarks (Tacloban, Samar, Leyte, Catarman, Biliran, San Juanico Bridge).
  - **Cultural Cuisine:** Binagol from Dagami, Salukara pancake from Samar, Chocolate Moron, Natong (gabi leaves).
  - **Vocabulary & Grammar:** Vocabulary translations (*tubig*, *kalayo*, *tuna*, *plete*, *balay*, *sangkay*, *diri*).
  - **Assistant Persona:** Acknowledging fully offline status without internet, no access to live lottery/weather feeds, no claims of human emotion or eating food, and no claims of OpenAI authorship.

### 2.2 Medical Chatbot Dataset (`data/private/medical-chat-reviewed.csv`)
- **Dataset Attribution:** Curated from [`ruslanmv/ai-medical-chatbot`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ruslanmv/ai-medical-chatbot) created by **Ruslan Magana Vsevolodovna** ([@ruslanmv](https://huggingface.co/ruslanmv)). Detailed citations in [`docs/DATASET_CREDITS.md`](DATASET_CREDITS.md).
- **Location:** Ignored `data/private/medical-chat-reviewed.csv` (SHA-256: `4f42a68981eb0cb34a0f04223bd5785e6c26f4bc05540ff7789ab6b9db0b31c8`)

- **Pipeline:** Generated by `training/prepare_medical_data.py` (filters out truncated web-scraping artifacts, extracts frontline clinical consultations, and integrates bilingual Waray/English primary care pairs).
- **Prompt Isolation:** Zero prompt overlap between `train` and `test` splits (enforced and verified by `training/medical_cli.py check`).

> **Key Clinical Finding:** Raw teleconsultation datasets like `ruslanmv/ai-medical-chatbot` contain unrestricted doctor prescribing behavior (e.g. prescribing Amoxicillin, Verapamil, and high dosages directly). When deployed to an offline mobile assistant, this leads to prescription rule violations. The curated Waray OTC alignment in **Run 03** is significantly safer and more accurate (40% pass rate) because it strictly enforces non-prescription OTC boundaries.

## 5. How to Reproduce Locally

From the repository root with `.venv` active:

```powershell
# --- Waray-First Assistant Pipeline ---
# 1. Validate dataset format & split separation
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe training\local_cli.py check --data data\private\waray-reviewed.csv

# 2. Train 4-bit LoRA adapter with all linear projection layers adapted (Run 03)
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe training\local_cli.py train --data data\private\waray-reviewed.csv --baseline-review outputs\waray-baseline.csv --output outputs\waray-run-03 --epochs 4 --all-linear

# 3. Merge adapter into base weights
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe training\merge_adapter.py --adapter outputs\waray-run-03\adapter --output models\waray-sailor2-1b-v2-merged

# 4. Convert to GGUF
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe .venv\llama.cpp\convert_hf_to_gguf.py models\waray-sailor2-1b-v2-merged --outfile models\waray-chat-v2-q8_0.gguf --outtype q8_0

# --- Separate Medical Chatbot Pipeline (ruslanmv dataset) ---
# 1. Ingest and curate dataset from ruslanmv/ai-medical-chatbot
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe training\prepare_medical_data.py

# 2. Validate medical dataset
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe training\medical_cli.py check --data data\private\medical-chat-reviewed.csv

# 3. Train medical LoRA adapter
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe training\medical_cli.py train --data data\private\medical-chat-reviewed.csv --baseline-review outputs\medical-baseline.csv --output outputs\medical-run-01 --epochs 3 --all-linear --max-length 384

# 4. Merge medical adapter and export GGUF
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe training\merge_adapter.py --adapter outputs\medical-run-01\adapter --output models\medical-sailor2-1b-merged
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe .venv\llama.cpp\convert_hf_to_gguf.py models\medical-sailor2-1b-merged --outfile models\medical-chat-v1-q8_0.gguf --outtype q8_0
```
